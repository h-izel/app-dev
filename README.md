## MY FAVORITE SERIES :heartpulse:
My favorite series is **Hi Bye, Mama!**

The series is about:
Cha Yu‑ri has been a ghost for five years after dying while pregnant. 
A divine “reincarnation project” gives her 49 days to return as a living human and resume her role as wife and mother. 
If she fails, she must move on to the afterlife. 

*I like this series because:*
- There are no villains
- The plot is GOOD!
- Bawled my eyes out :sob:
- Made me realize that we should cherish life

## The MAIN CHARACTERS are:

| Characters | Description | 
| ---------- | ----------- |
| Cha Yu‑ri | A loving mother who returns from the dead for 49 days. |
| Cho Gang‑hwa | Her husband, a surgeon struggling with trauma and guilt. |
| Oh Min‑jung | Gang‑hwa’s new wife, a kind stepmother to Yu‑ri’s daughter. |
